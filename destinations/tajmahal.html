Taj Mahal with TripMaster

History

The Taj Mahal, located in Agra, India, is one of the most iconic monuments in the world. Built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal, it is a symbol of love and architectural brilliance. The monument is renowned for its stunning white marble facade, intricate inlay work, and beautiful gardens. The Taj Mahal is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and attracts millions of visitors each year.

Location

The Taj Mahal is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. It is situated on the banks of the Yamuna River, approximately 2.5 km from the Agra Fort and 6 km from the Agra Railway Station.

How to Reach

The nearest airport is Agra Airport (AGR), which is about 7 km away from the Taj Mahal. You can also reach Agra by train, with regular services from major cities like Delhi, Jaipur, and Mumbai. Once in Agra, you can hire a taxi or use local transportation to reach the monument.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit the Taj Mahal is during the winter months (October to March) when the weather is pleasant and ideal for sightseeing. The monument is particularly beautiful during sunrise and sunset, when the marble reflects different hues.

Things to Do

  • Explore the gardens surrounding the Taj Mahal.
  • Visit the Agra Fort, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Take a boat ride on the Yamuna River for a unique view of the Taj Mahal.
  • Enjoy local cuisine at nearby restaurants.
  • Shop for souvenirs and handicrafts in Agra.

Local Cuisine

Agra is famous for its Mughlai cuisine. Don't miss trying the following dishes:

  • Agra Petha - A sweet made from ash gourd.
  • Mughlai Biryani - A fragrant rice dish with spices and meat.
  • Paratha - A stuffed flatbread, often served with yogurt and pickles.
  • Chaat - A popular street food snack.
  • Paneer Tikka - Grilled cottage cheese marinated in spices.

Entry Fees

The entry fee for Indian citizens is INR 50, while foreign tourists are charged INR 1,000. Children under the age of 15 can enter for free. Additional charges may apply for photography and videography.

Opening Hours

The Taj Mahal is open from sunrise to sunset, except on Fridays when it is closed for prayers. The best time to visit is early morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds and enjoy the beautiful lighting.

Travel Tips

  • Dress modestly and respectfully when visiting the Taj Mahal.
  • Carry a water bottle and stay hydrated, especially during summer.
  • Be cautious of pickpockets in crowded areas.
  • Hire a local guide for a more informative experience.
  • Plan your visit early in the morning to avoid crowds.
  • Check the official website for any updates on entry fees and timings.
  • Consider visiting during the full moon for a magical experience.
  • Respect the rules and regulations of the monument.
  • Take your time to explore the intricate details of the architecture.
