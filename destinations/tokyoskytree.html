Tokyo Skytree with TripMaster

History

Tokyo Skytree was completed in 2012 and stands as the tallest structure in Japan and the second tallest in the world. It serves as a broadcasting tower, observation deck, and major tourist attraction in Tokyo.

Location

Tokyo Skytree is located in Sumida, Tokyo, Japan. It is a prominent landmark visible throughout the city skyline.

How to Reach

Take the Tokyo Metro Hanzomon Line to Oshiage Station. The tower is just a few minutes’ walk from there. Alternatively, taxis and buses are also available.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is late afternoon, so you can enjoy both daylight views and sunset/night illumination from the top. Spring and autumn offer the clearest skies.

Things to Do

  • Visit the Tembo Deck and Galleria for breathtaking views.
  • Explore the Skytree Town shopping complex.
  • Dine at the Sky Restaurant 634.
  • Check out the Sumida Aquarium and Planetarium.
  • Shop for exclusive Tokyo Skytree souvenirs.

Local Cuisine

Try delicious Japanese foods in Skytree Town such as:

  • Ramen and sushi combos
  • Matcha desserts
  • Takoyaki and tempura
  • Sky Restaurant Kaiseki meals
  • Sweet potato treats from local vendors

Entry Fees

Entry fee to the Tembo Deck is around ₹1500, and an additional fee applies to access the higher Galleria level.

Opening Hours

Open daily from 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Last admission is 1 hour before closing time.

Travel Tips

  • Buy tickets online to skip the queue.
  • Visit on a weekday for fewer crowds.
  • Check weather apps before going for best views.
  • Carry yen in cash for small vendors.

Safety Tips

  • Follow tower safety protocols and stay behind railings.
  • Keep an eye on your belongings in the shopping areas.
  • Stay hydrated, especially in summer months.
