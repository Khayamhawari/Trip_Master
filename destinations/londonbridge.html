London Bridge with TripMaster

History

The London Bridge has existed in various forms since the Roman period. The current version, opened in 1973, replaced a 19th-century stone-arched bridge, and is an important part of the city’s rich heritage and infrastructure.

Location

London Bridge is situated in Central London and spans the River Thames, connecting the City of London with Southwark.

How to Reach

You can reach London Bridge via the London Underground (Northern and Jubilee lines) or by national rail services stopping at London Bridge station. It's also accessible via buses and Thames Riverboats.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is from April to October when the weather is milder. Evenings offer beautiful views of the lit-up skyline and Thames reflections.

Things to Do

  • Walk across the historic bridge.
  • Visit the nearby Tower Bridge Exhibition.
  • Explore Borough Market for food and local goods.
  • Take a river cruise along the Thames.
  • Enjoy panoramic views from The Shard.

Local Cuisine

London offers a blend of global flavors and British classics. Nearby must-tries include:

  • Fish and Chips
  • English Breakfast
  • Pie and Mash
  • Street Food at Borough Market
  • Afternoon Tea at The Shard

Entry Fees

Walking across London Bridge is free. Attractions nearby like The Shard and Tower Bridge have separate entry tickets.

Opening Hours

Accessible 24/7 for pedestrians. Public transport options vary between 5:00 AM to midnight.

Travel Tips

  • Carry a contactless card for public transport.
  • Book major attraction tickets online in advance.
  • Carry an umbrella—London weather is unpredictable!
  • Use walking tours for historic insights.

Safety Tips

  • Stay alert in crowded public areas.
  • Keep belongings close and secure.
  • Follow pedestrian signals and traffic rules strictly.
