Niagara Falls with TripMaster

History

Niagara Falls has captivated travelers for centuries. Formed during the last Ice Age, the falls are one of the world's most powerful and iconic natural wonders, attracting millions of visitors each year since the 18th century.

Location

Niagara Falls is located on the border of Ontario, Canada, and New York, USA. It comprises three waterfalls: Horseshoe Falls, American Falls, and Bridal Veil Falls.

How to Reach

Niagara Falls is accessible via Buffalo Niagara International Airport (USA) or Toronto Pearson International Airport (Canada). From there, take a bus, rental car, or train to the falls.

Best Time to Visit

Visit between May and September for the best views and attractions like boat tours and light shows. Winter brings a different charm with frozen falls and fewer crowds.

Things to Do

  • Take the Maid of the Mist boat ride.
  • Experience the Journey Behind the Falls.
  • Enjoy Niagara SkyWheel views.
  • Walk along the Niagara Parkway.
  • Visit the Butterfly Conservatory.

Local Cuisine

Niagara Falls offers a variety of international and North American dishes. Don't miss:

  • Canadian Poutine
  • New York-style Pizza
  • Maple-glazed Donuts
  • Niagara Icewine
  • Street food at Clifton Hill

Entry Fees

Viewing the falls is free. However, attractions like boat rides, observation decks, and tours have separate entry fees.

Opening Hours

The area is open 24/7. Most attractions operate between 9:00 AM and 10:00 PM, depending on the season.

Travel Tips

  • Carry your passport for border crossing (if visiting both sides).
  • Bring waterproof gear for boat rides.
  • Use a guided tour for an all-in-one experience.
  • Stay overnight to enjoy night illuminations.

Safety Tips

  • Follow all safety signs near viewing points.
  • Keep valuables secure, especially in crowded areas.
  • Check weather forecasts before outdoor plans.
