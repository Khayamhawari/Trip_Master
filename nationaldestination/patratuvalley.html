Patratu Valley with TripMaster

Overview

Patratu Valley, located in Jharkhand, is a picturesque destination famous for its lush green hills, winding roads, and serene lake views. Surrounded by forests and nestled in the lap of nature, the valley offers a perfect escape from city life. It is known for its scenic beauty, especially the drive through the Patratu Ghat with hairpin turns and panoramic views.

Location

Patratu Valley is located in Ramgarh district, Jharkhand, around 40 km from the state capital Ranchi. It is easily accessible by road and serves as a weekend getaway for locals and tourists alike.

How to Reach

You can reach Ranchi by train or flight. From Ranchi, you can hire a cab or take a local bus to Patratu. The nearest airport is Birsa Munda Airport, and the nearest railway station is Ranchi Junction.

Best Time to Visit

October to March is the best time to visit Patratu Valley as the weather is cool and ideal for sightseeing, boating, and photography.

Things to Do

  • Enjoy scenic drive through Patratu Ghat
  • Boating in Patratu Lake
  • Sunset photography and nature walks
  • Visit Patratu Dam
  • Picnic near the valley viewpoints

Local Cuisine

The valley has small eateries and dhabas offering local Jharkhandi and Indian meals like:

  • Handia (rice beer - local specialty)
  • Litti-Chokha
  • Pittha
  • Chana Samosa & Jalebi

Entry Fees

There is no entry fee to visit Patratu Valley. However, separate charges apply for boating and parking.

Timings

The valley is open all day. Boating facilities operate from 9 AM to 5 PM. Early morning and late afternoon are best for views.

Travel Tips

  • Start early to enjoy the sunrise drive
  • Carry your own snacks and water
  • Use Google Maps navigation
  • Don't litter, help keep the valley clean

Safety Tips

  • Drive carefully on the hilly ghat roads
  • Stay in groups during nature walks
  • Keep phone charged for maps and photos
  • Respect local culture and nature
