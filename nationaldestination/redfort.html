Red Fort with TripMaster

History

The Red Fort, located in Delhi, India, is a historic fortification built in the 17th century by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan. It served as the main residence of the emperors for around 200 years. The fort is a stunning example of Mughal architecture and is a symbol of India's rich heritage and independence, as it is where the Prime Minister hoists the national flag every Independence Day.

Location

The Red Fort is situated in the heart of Old Delhi, near Chandni Chowk. It is easily accessible by metro, with the nearest station being Lal Qila Metro Station on the Violet Line.

How to Reach

Delhi is well-connected by air, train, and road. You can reach the Red Fort by taking the Delhi Metro or hiring a cab or rickshaw. It is just 20 km from the Indira Gandhi International Airport and 3.5 km from New Delhi Railway Station.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is from October to March, when the weather is pleasant. Avoid peak summer months due to extreme heat.

Things to Do

  • Explore the historical architecture and museums within the fort.
  • Attend the evening light and sound show.
  • Walk through Chandni Chowk for shopping and food.
  • Visit Jama Masjid nearby.
  • Enjoy local Delhi street food around the area.

Local Cuisine

Delhi offers diverse flavors. Must-try dishes include:

  • Butter Chicken
  • Chole Bhature
  • Jalebi and Rabri
  • Kebabs and Paranthas
  • Street Chaat

Entry Fees

Entry fee for Indian citizens is INR 35 and for foreign tourists is INR 500. Entry is free for children below 15 years of age. Additional charges apply for the light and sound show.

Opening Hours

The Red Fort is open daily from 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM. It remains closed on Mondays.

Travel Tips

  • Carry a government ID for entry.
  • Hire a certified guide or use an audio guide for better insights.
  • Visit early to avoid crowds and heat.
  • Respect monument rules and avoid littering.
  • Keep emergency contacts saved.

Safety Tips

  • Stay alert in crowded areas.
  • Use only official or pre-booked transportation.
  • Keep valuables safe and avoid carrying too much cash.
  • Follow local COVID and security regulations.
