Manali with TripMaster

Overview

Manali, nestled in Himachal Pradesh, is a popular hill station known for its breathtaking views of snow-capped mountains, adventure sports, and vibrant cafes. A perfect blend of nature and modern vibes, Manali draws tourists year-round for skiing, river rafting, and mountain trekking.

Location

Manali is located in the Kullu Valley of Himachal Pradesh, about 530 km from Delhi. It lies along the Beas River and offers spectacular views of the surrounding Himalayas.

How to Reach

You can reach Manali by overnight bus or taxi from Delhi/Chandigarh. The nearest airport is Bhuntar, about 50 km away. The nearest railway station is Joginder Nagar, but better connectivity is from Chandigarh.

Best Time to Visit

October to February for snow lovers, and March to June for pleasant weather and sightseeing. Avoid monsoon due to landslides.

Things to Do

  • Visit Solang Valley and Rohtang Pass
  • Try paragliding and snow sports
  • Explore Hidimba Temple and Manu Temple
  • Relax in Vashisht Hot Water Springs
  • Stroll through Old Manali cafes

Local Cuisine

Manali offers a wide range of Himachali and international food. Must-tries include:

  • Siddu (steamed bread with fillings)
  • Thukpa and Momos
  • Madra and Chana Madra
  • Butter Tea and Tibetan dishes

Entry Fees

No general entry fee for Manali. Rohtang Pass requires a permit (~₹500), and adventure sports have individual charges.

Timings

Open year-round. Major attractions operate between 9 AM and 6 PM.

Travel Tips

  • Carry warm clothes, even in summer
  • Book Rohtang permits in advance
  • Cash is preferred in remote areas
  • Watch for road conditions in winter

Safety Tips

  • Avoid isolated trails alone
  • Respect local customs and nature
  • Stay alert during snow activities
  • Keep emergency contacts handy
