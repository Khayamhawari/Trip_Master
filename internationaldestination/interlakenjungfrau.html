Interlaken & Jungfrau with TripMaster

History

Interlaken is a famous Swiss town located between two beautiful lakes, offering breathtaking views of the Bernese Alps. Jungfrau, one of the highest peaks in Europe, is accessible via the Jungfrau Railway, and attracts visitors year-round with its glaciers and alpine beauty.

Location

Interlaken is located in the Bernese Oberland region of Switzerland, nestled between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz. Jungfrau is situated in the heart of the Bernese Alps, accessible from Interlaken by a scenic train ride.

How to Reach

Interlaken is easily accessible by train from major cities like Zurich, Geneva, or Lucerne. The Jungfrau Railway departs from Interlaken and takes visitors to the Jungfraujoch, the highest railway station in Europe.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Interlaken and Jungfrau is during the summer months (June to September) when the weather is pleasant, and the views are clear. Winter offers excellent skiing opportunities, especially at Jungfrau.

Things to Do

  • Take a scenic train ride to Jungfraujoch, the highest railway station in Europe.
  • Explore the Ice Palace and Sphinx Observatory at Jungfrau.
  • Enjoy paragliding and adventure sports in Interlaken.
  • Go hiking or skiing in the surrounding mountain trails.
  • Relax by the lakes in Interlaken and take boat cruises.

Local Cuisine

Swiss cuisine is known for its rich and hearty flavors. In Interlaken, you can enjoy:

  • Fondue (melted cheese served with bread and vegetables)
  • Rösti (Swiss-style fried potatoes)
  • Swiss chocolate and pastries
  • Älplermagronen (Swiss pasta dish with potatoes, cheese, and onions)

Entry Fees

Admission to Jungfrau is priced at ₹8000 for adults. There are additional charges for special experiences like the Ice Palace and the Sphinx Observatory.

Opening Hours

The Jungfrau Railway operates from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM daily, with extended hours during peak seasons. Interlaken is a year-round destination.

Travel Tips

  • Book your tickets to Jungfrau in advance to avoid long queues.
  • Dress in layers, as temperatures at Jungfrau can be much colder than in Interlaken.
  • Carry cash in Swiss Francs (CHF) for small purchases.
  • Be prepared for altitude changes, especially at Jungfrau (3500 meters above sea level).

Safety Tips

  • Follow all safety guidelines at Jungfrau and during adventure activities.
  • Stay hydrated, especially if engaging in outdoor activities like hiking.
  • Wear sturdy shoes for hiking and walking on snow.
